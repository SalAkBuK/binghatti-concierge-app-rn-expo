2. Investigation begins immediately
3. Hotfix deployed if confirmed

1. Acknowledgment within 4 hours
2. Investigation within 24 hours
3. Fix in next update cycle
4. Status update within 48 hours

### Medium Priority (1 week)

1. Acknowledgment within 24 hours
2. Planned for upcoming sprint
3. Status update weekly

### Low Priority (Backlog)

1. Acknowledgment within 48 hours
2. Added to feature backlog
3. Considered for future releases
